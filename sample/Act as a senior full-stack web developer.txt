### 1. BACKGROUND & CANNON MECHANICS
- Cannon Type: 180° Lazy-Accelerated Ender Pearl Cannon built on the Nether Roof.
- Launch Coordinates (Origin): X = 8.49, Y = 241.00, Z = 15.64.
- Facing Direction: Facing North (Negative Z axis).
- Controls:
  - Control A (Dropper count): Pushes the pearl East (Positive X) to steer and correct direction.
  - Control B (Dropper count): Drives main forward thrust North (Negative Z) and introduces inherent Westward (Negative X) drift.
- Hardware Constraint:
  - The redstone circuit caps Control A based on B: A_effective = min(A_set, B_set + 2).
  - Any items in A above (B + 2) remain un-dispensed in the dropper.
- Y-Level Behavior:
  - Pearls land at Y = ~128.00 (or ~128.61) because they collide with the Nether bedrock ceiling on their downward trajectory.
- TNT Duplication Speed:
  - 1 TNT dupes every 9 frames at 30 FPS = 0.300 seconds per TNT dupe.

### 3. MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS
- Delta Z (Forward Thrust): ΔZ = -105.26 * B + 519.24
- Delta X (Lateral Steering): ΔX = 152.17 * A_effective - 80.85 * B + 12.89
- Target Z: Z_landing = 15.64 + ΔZ
- Target X: X_landing = 8.49 + ΔX
- Target Y: Y_landing = 128.00 (Fixed nether roof floor collision)
- Firing Angle (Degrees relative to North): Angle = arctan2(ΔX, -ΔZ) * (180 / π)
- Total Distance: Dist = sqrt(ΔX² + ΔZ²)
- Loading Time (s): t_load = 7.5 + 0.3 * B
- Waiting Time (s): t_wait = 16.1 + 0.55 * B
