Summary

  • The controls are the other way round from your notes. North–south range depends only on B: B=64 landed at z −5908.87 in four shots (A=1, 10, 64, 128) and B=32 at −2690.13 twice. A only slides the landing sideways (east–west).
  • A is linear steering. At B=64 each A item moves the landing 144.14 blocks sideways. Straight north is A=(B+1)/2. A is capped at B+1: extra items are ignored and the dropper drains min(A, B+2).
  • The fan is ±38.3°, not 180°. tan θ = 0.7906·(2A−n)/n with n=B+1. It is mirror-symmetric (B=64: A=1 → −4533, A=65 → +4693) but never wider at any B. Each TNT behaves like a push 38.3° off north.
  • Range: the pearl leaves at a speed proportional to n, about 2.4° upward, then follows normal pearl physics (drag 0.99, gravity 0.03) down 113 blocks to y=128. Range per TNT climbs from 69 to 104 blocks and saturates because drag caps horizontal reach near 100× launch speed. Above B≈200 it is simply ≈104 blocks per TNT.
  • Loading time = 6.6 s + 0.3 s × (B+1), matching your measured 0.300 s per dupe, independent of A.
  • Waiting time is mostly the pearl crossing new chunks and is only roughly predictable (±25 s). 2 of 4 attempts at B≥384 failed and only worked on retry; B=1 failed.

Model accuracy on landing spot: The yaw/pitch numbers in your tp commands are your camera, not the pearl, so I ignored them. Nether ×8 = overworld coordinates.

flight time T solves   100·(0.043n + 3)·(1 − 0.99^T) − 3T = −113 ;   F = 1 − 0.99^T

What to test next

  • A=0 and A=B+1 at B=10 or 20: confirms the mirrored ±38.3° limits and whether A=0 works.
  • A well above B+1 at small B (e.g. A=30, B=5): confirms the cap and the B+2 drain.
  • B=1 to 8, one shot each: checks the "+1" (n=B+1) and finds the smallest working B. Also repeat B=64, A=33 twice to see if shots are deterministic.
  • B≈100 and B≈200, plus a mid A at B=256+: fills the curve where range per TNT is still rising.
  • For timing, log three times per shot: TNT finished loading, you stepped in, you teleported. Use fresh directions, since re-used chunks are faster.
  • If you really need 180°, the current build cannot do it; check what stops A beyond B+1 (the direction limit) and rebuild that part.
